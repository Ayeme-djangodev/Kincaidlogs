Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and any other details you provide during registration. When you make a purchase or fund your wallet, our payment processor handles your payment details directly - we do not store your card or bank information ourselves.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate your account, process orders and payments, deliver purchased assets, communicate with you about your orders, and improve the platform. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Payment Processing

Wallet funding and payments are processed through a third-party payment provider. Your payment details are handled entirely by that provider under their own security and privacy standards - we only receive confirmation of successful or failed transactions.

4. Data Security

Sensitive account credentials associated with purchased listings are encrypted at rest and only ever revealed to the buyer after a completed purchase. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no online platform can guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies & Sessions

We use session cookies to keep you logged in and to remember short-lived preferences, such as whether you've acknowledged our terms and conditions. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us through the support channels listed on our site.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of KincaidLogs after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
