About KincaidLogs

KincaidLogs is a marketplace for buying and selling verified social media accounts, websites, domains, and other digital assets - built for people who want a safer, more transparent way to trade digital property.

Verified Listings

Every listing goes through a review process before it's published, so buyers know what they're getting.

Escrow Protection

Funds are held securely until delivery is confirmed, protecting both buyers and sellers from disputes.

Fast Support

Reach us directly through WhatsApp or Telegram for quick help with orders, disputes, or account questions.

Our Mission

Buying and selling digital accounts has traditionally happened in unregulated corners of the internet - group chats, forums, and direct messages with no protection if something goes wrong. KincaidLogs exists to change that, by bringing structure, verification, and buyer protection to a market that badly needed it.
